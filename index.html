如果我们把树与周边事物产生的联系视作一种标记，那么相片的矩形边框就充当了一种机械的编排机制，而树与物作为标记进入相框的方式则成为了一种全新的未知的语言，一种在高度商业化、机能化的人工自然背景下的人为的环境语言，一种利用树与物视觉行为关系的逻辑、语言、和意义创造出的独特的戏剧。 语言并不像呼吸是我们与生俱来的本领，而是我们后天学习来的，像婴儿咿呀学语一样，模仿是一切语言学习的开始。树上张贴的宣传单、倚靠着树的单车、树梢上的垃圾......我通过模仿树与周边物象的关系得到最基本的单词，再利用特定“语法”将这些单词进行重构得出不同的可能性。 如果将所看到的树周边的“视觉行为”加以理解，我们就能将其转译成为人与人的行为关系。再经过裁切解构过的人体已然分辨不出是什么部位或正在进行什么活动，流动在相框中的只剩下缺失的、被遮蔽的黑与白。

If we view the connections a tree forms with its surroundings as a form of signage, then the rectangular frame of the photograph acts as a mechanical organizing mechanism. The way the tree and these objects enter the frame as signs gives rise to a novel, uncharted language—an artificial environmental language existing against a backdrop of highly commercialized, functionalized "man-made nature." It is a unique drama created through the logic, language, and meaning inherent in the visual interplay between the tree and the objects around it. Language is not an innate ability like breathing; it is something we acquire. Much like an infant’s babbling, imitation marks the beginning of all language learning. Flyers plastered on a trunk, a bicycle leaning against the bark, litter caught in the upper branches—by imitating the relationships between the tree and the surrounding imagery, I gather "basic words," which I then reconstruct using a specific "grammar" to generate a range of possibilities. If we interpret the "visual behaviors" observed around the tree, we can translate them into the dynamics of human interaction. Yet, once the human form has been cropped and deconstructed, it becomes impossible to distinguish specific body parts or actions; all that remains flowing within the frame are fragments of black and white—shapes defined by absence and concealment.

从植物和大气的关系出发，我们希望讨论的是人类活动对自然产生的影响。回顾过往的人类发展历史不难发现，“人类中心主义”是我们长久以来形成的一种现代观，这在自然界以一种专制主义的资源垄断来体现，随之而来的生态危机挑战了我们关于可持续性的理解。其中，最显著的生态危机之一就是大气污染，而其直接取决于森林资源的减少与破坏。结合我们对木材资源转化的思辨，我们创作了艺术装置《1/∞》。此作品中包含两层交换关系，分别指代从材料到制品的转化与活动中的气体消耗。我们希望以此凸显自然界中不对等的滥用与专制，让观者产生关于人类、植物与大气关系平衡的反思。

By examining the relationship between plants and the atmosphere, we aim to explore the impact of human activity on the natural world. A look back at human history reveals the long-standing prevalence of "anthropocentrism"—a modern mindset that manifests as a form of despotic resource monopolization—and the ensuing ecological crises that challenge our understanding of sustainability. Air pollution stands out as one of the most significant of these crises, driven directly by the depletion and destruction of forest resources. Drawing on our critical reflections regarding the transformation of timber resources, we created the art installation *1/∞*. The work embodies two layers of exchange: the transformation of raw materials into finished products and the consumption of gases during these processes. Through this piece, we seek to highlight the inequitable exploitation and authoritarian nature of human interaction with the environment, prompting viewers to reflect on the balance between humanity, plants, and the atmosphere.

《严禁》探讨规则与违规、秩序与失序之间模糊的边界。书是规则的载体，却以规范方式讲述违规；举报广告作为插页进入后，反而成为书中的违规物。

全书三层：

1. 图像层：乱贴、乱画、乱刻等满版编排，“严禁”用红色，末章以撕、涂、覆盖直至清空。 2. 举报层：目录由电话构成，读者通过十个举报电话寻找书中十处隐藏内容。 3. 文本层：以涂抹、覆盖、字体变化，及倾斜、重复、叠压，实践“贴、写、盖、画、刻”的失序。

书中并置两种“严禁”：针对个人的规则简单易执行；针对权力的规则模糊难执行，甚至违规的正是规则本身。二者都是宣传，只是私人业务与国家政治之别。设计不提供答案，只把矛盾转化为可翻阅、可互动的视觉经验。

*DO NOT* explores the blurred boundaries between rules and violations, order and disorder. While the book serves as a vehicle for rules, it recounts acts of transgression through a standardized format; conversely, the inclusion of "reporting advertisements" as inserts transforms them into the very objects of violation within the book.

The book comprises three layers:

1. The Visual Layer: Features full-page layouts of unauthorized posting, scribbling, and carving. The phrase "Strictly Prohibited" appears in red, while the final chapter depicts a process of tearing, painting over, and masking until the pages are completely blank. 2. The Reporting Layer: The table of contents consists of telephone numbers; readers use these ten reporting lines to locate ten pieces of hidden content within the book. 3. The Textual Layer: Employs techniques such as obscuring, masking, and varying fonts— alongside tilting, repetition, and layering—to enact the disorder associated with unauthorized posting, writing, stamping, drawing, and carving.

The book juxtaposes two types of prohibitions: rules targeting individuals are simple and easy to enforce, whereas rules targeting power are ambiguous and difficult to enforce—often, it is the rules themselves that constitute the violation. Both serve as forms of propaganda, differing only in that one concerns private enterprise while the other involves state politics. The design offers no definitive answers; instead, it transforms these contradictions into a visual experience that invites browsing and interaction.

这是一本关于阿勒泰门的百科全书，不同时空作品直观的将阿勒泰的门置于同一页面中。 Tracing的主题是关于时间与社群对空间的塑形，这种塑形把互联网图片视作成某种有趣的“遗产”，书中充满真实照片与互联网图片的矛盾感。门似乎是一种象征，如果说建筑与生命有关的话，那么门就极富生命力。门的变化取决于个体，也取决于当地的社群。它们被增加，被改造，被抛弃，被涂鸦……这种“趣味”，再引入时间与互联网所产生的“定格感”，反映了阿勒泰社区人群与环境之间的相互作用，以及线上与线下的相互作用与补偿。 全书一共100个条目，每个条目为对页图片展示，分别在三个时间线中对比了线上与线下的情况。环境与光线的对比的插页贯穿全书。

This is an encyclopedia of doors in Altay; it intuitively places doors from different times and spaces side-by-side on the same page. The theme of *Tracing* explores how time and community shape space—a process that treats internet images as a form of intriguing "heritage." The book is imbued with a tension between authentic photographs and images sourced from the internet. The door serves as a symbol; if architecture is linked to life, then the door is brimming with vitality. These doors evolve based on the actions of individuals and the local community—being added, modified, abandoned, or covered in graffiti. This "playfulness," combined with the "sense of a frozen moment" created by the interplay of time and the internet, reflects the dynamic relationship between the people of Altay and their environment, as well as the interplay and mutual compensation between the online and offline worlds. The book comprises 100 entries, each presented as a double-page spread that compares online and offline realities across three distinct timelines. Inserts contrasting environments and lighting conditions are interspersed throughout the volume.
